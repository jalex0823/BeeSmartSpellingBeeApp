# Accessibility and COPPA/Designed for Families Checklist

## Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA‑aligned)
- Color contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for text; ≥ 3:1 for large text and UI components
- Scalable text: respects OS font size; avoid truncation
- Screen reader labels: meaningful labels for buttons, mic icon, and OCR controls
- Focus order and visible focus states; full keyboard navigation in web views
- Touch targets ≥ 44×44 pt (iOS) / 48×48 dp (Android)
- Motion/animation: reduce motion settings honored; avoid distracting loops
- Error messages and hints are conveyed with text (not color alone)

## COPPA / Kids Category (Apple) & Designed for Families (Google)
- No third‑party ads; no behavioral tracking
- Parental gate for: external links, purchases, account sign‑up, or leaving the app
- Do not request personal info from children; accounts (if any) limited to adults
- Clear privacy disclosures; provide contact for parents
- Data minimization: collect only what’s necessary for features (uploads, OCR, voice)
- Voice and images processed only for the feature; avoid retention of raw media

## Data Safety and Privacy Forms
- iOS Privacy “nutrition labels”:
  - Tracking: None
  - Data Linked to You: account info (if enabled), user content (uploads)
  - Not Linked to You: diagnostics (optional)
- Google Play Data Safety:
  - Collected: user content; optional account info; diagnostics
  - Sharing: none to third parties
  - Encrypted in transit; deletion available upon request

## Review‑Ready Items
- Privacy Policy URL and Terms URL in app and store listings
- In‑app About/Help page with version, support email, links
- Gated external links (parental gate)
- Tested with VoiceOver (iOS) and TalkBack (Android)
