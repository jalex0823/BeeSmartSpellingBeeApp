# Grades Hidden Until First Quiz — Validation Across All Account Types

Grades (letter grade and GPA) are hidden until stats are generated by the first completed quiz. This is implemented everywhere that letter grade / cumulative GPA is shown, for **all account types**: Student, Teacher, Parent, Admin.

**Condition used everywhere:** `(total_quizzes_completed or 0) > 0`

---

## Where Letter Grade / GPA Is Shown (and Gated)

| Template | Who sees it | Condition | Status |
|----------|-------------|-----------|--------|
| **unified_menu.html** | **All account types** (Student, Teacher, Parent, Admin) when visiting `/` or `/app` | `(current_user.total_quizzes_completed or 0) > 0` | ✅ Grade card not rendered until first quiz |
| **auth/student_dashboard.html** | **Students** (student dashboard after login) | `(current_user.total_quizzes_completed or 0) > 0` | ✅ Cumulative Grade + GPA (0–4.0) cards not rendered until first quiz |
| **admin/dashboard.html** | **Admins** (admin dashboard, “My Students” table) | `((student.total_quizzes_completed or 0) > 0) or ((student.quiz_count or 0) > 0)` | ✅ Grade (GPA) cell shows "—" until student has quizzes |
| **teacher/student_detail.html** | **Teachers & Parents** (when viewing a linked student) | `(student.total_quizzes_completed or 0) > 0` | ✅ GPA stat and Best Grade line not rendered until first quiz |

---

## Account-Type Summary

### Student
- **Student dashboard** (`/auth/dashboard`): Cumulative Grade and GPA cards hidden until `(current_user.total_quizzes_completed or 0) > 0`. ✅
- **Home menu** (`/`, `/app`): Grade stat card hidden until same condition. ✅

### Teacher
- **Home menu** (`/`, `/app`): Grade stat card hidden until `(current_user.total_quizzes_completed or 0) > 0`. ✅
- **Teacher dashboard** (`/teacher/dashboard`): No letter grade/GPA column (only “Grade” = grade level, e.g. 5th grade). Nothing to hide. ✅
- **Teacher student detail** (`/teacher/student/<id>`): GPA and Best Grade hidden until `(student.total_quizzes_completed or 0) > 0`. ✅

### Parent
- **Home menu** (`/`, `/app`): Grade stat card hidden until same condition. ✅
- **Parent dashboard** (`/parent/dashboard`): No letter grade/GPA column (only “Grade” = grade level). Nothing to hide. ✅
- **Student detail** (same template as teacher: `/teacher/student/<id>`): GPA and Best Grade hidden until first quiz. ✅

### Admin
- **Home menu** (`/`, `/app`): Grade stat card hidden until same condition. ✅
- **Admin dashboard** (`/admin/dashboard`): Per-student Grade (GPA) cell shows "—" until student has at least one quiz (or quiz_count > 0). ✅
- **Admin user detail** (`/admin/users/<id>`): No letter grade/GPA displayed; no change needed. ✅

---

## JavaScript / Dynamic Updates

- **Student dashboard:** Stats refresh only updates/shows grade elements when `quizzesCompleted > 0`; otherwise hides grade cards.
- **Unified menu:** `applyStats()` only updates grade elements when `quizzesCompleted > 0`; grade card is not in the DOM when no quizzes (server-side).
- **Admin dashboard:** `updateRow()` shows "—" for grade when `quizzesCompleted === 0`.
- **Teacher student detail:** Polling shows GPA stat only when `quizzesCompleted > 0`; hides when 0.

---

## Conclusion

The change **“grades hidden until stats are generated by first quiz”** is implemented **across all account types** (Student, Teacher, Parent, Admin) in every template and view that displays letter grade or cumulative GPA.
