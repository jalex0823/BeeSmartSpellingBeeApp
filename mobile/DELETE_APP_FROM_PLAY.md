# Remove the app from Google Play

**Nothing live in the store?** You can delete the app from your Play Console account so it's gone and you can start fresh (e.g. new app with a new key) if you want.

---

## Delete the app from your account

1. Go to [Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
2. Open **BeeSmartSpelling** (or go to **All apps** and click the app).
3. In the **left menu**, go to **Settings** (or **Setup** → **App settings**, or the **⋮** three-dots on the app card if you're on All apps).
4. Scroll for **Delete app** or **Remove app** or **Account and settings** → delete.
5. Confirm. The app is removed from your developer account.

**If you don't see Delete app:** Try **All apps** → click BeeSmartSpelling → **Settings** in the left sidebar, or look under **Policy** / **App content** for any "Remove" or "Delete" option. Google sometimes moves it.

---

## If you had a live release (for reference)

Only needed if the app was ever on the store: **Production** in the left menu → **Unpublish app**, then delete as above. Since you don't have one live, skip unpublish and just delete.

---

Your project code stays on your machine; this only removes the app from Play Console so you can create a new one (e.g. same code, new app listing, new key) if you want.
