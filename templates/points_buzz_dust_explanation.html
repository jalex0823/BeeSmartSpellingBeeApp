🐝 Points & Buzz Dust

How Your Score Works in BeeSmart

🧠 What Are Points?

Points show how well you spelled the words in each quiz.

Teachers and parents use Points to understand grades and progress.

Points are based on:

  • Correct answers
  • Question difficulty
  • Quiz length
Points = your school score.
They help measure learning, not game rank.

✨ What Is Buzz Dust?

Buzz Dust is magic XP you earn while learning.

Every time you spell words, you generate Buzz Dust for the hive.

Buzz Dust is used to:

  • Unlock Bee Classes (ranks)
  • Earn badges & avatars
  • Climb the leaderboards
Buzz Dust = your game progress.
It makes learning feel like an adventure.

🐝 Do Points and Buzz Dust Affect Each Other?

Points stay separate for grades and GPA.

Buzz Dust is extra and used only for ranks and rewards.

Scenario What It Means
High Buzz Dust + High Points You're practicing a lot AND doing great! 🌟
Great Grades + Modest Buzz Dust Excellent work, but fewer quizzes taken
Huge Buzz Dust + Mixed Grades Lots of practice! Keep it up to improve grades

Both are good—and both help you grow.
