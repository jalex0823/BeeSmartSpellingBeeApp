Frequently Asked Questions 🤔

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes! You can cancel your subscription anytime from your iPhone settings. No questions asked. If you cancel, you'll keep premium access until the end of your billing period.
How does the Family plan work?
With Family Sharing enabled, up to 6 family members can share one subscription. Each member gets their own account, progress tracking, and avatar customization. Perfect for households with multiple kids!
Is there a free trial?
Yes! All new subscribers get a 7-day free trial to test out all premium features before committing. You won't be charged until the trial ends.
What happens to my avatars if I downgrade?
If you cancel or downgrade, you'll lose access to premium avatars but keep all your progress and unlocked achievements. You can always re-subscribe to get them back!
Which plan should I choose?
If you have multiple kids or family members learning spelling, go with Family ($7.99/mo). For individual learners committed to year-round practice, Yearly ($39.99/yr) saves you $20. Monthly ($4.99/mo) is great for trying it out!
Are payments secure?
Absolutely! All payments are processed through Apple's secure payment system. We never see or store your payment information.
