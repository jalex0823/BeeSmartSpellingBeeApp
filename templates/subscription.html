Frequently Asked Questions 🤔

Yes! You can cancel your subscription anytime from your iPhone settings. No questions asked. If you cancel, you'll keep premium access until the end of your billing period.

If you cancel or downgrade, you'll lose access to premium avatars but keep all your progress and unlocked achievements. You can always re-subscribe to get them back!
