# Google Play Console – Product IDs for BeeSmart Spelling Bee

Use this table when creating one-time products (avatars) and subscriptions in Google Play Console.

**Step 1 – Product ID:** Periods and underscores allowed. Use `beesmart.avatar.<slug>.v3` (same as iOS).

**Step 2 – Purchase option ID:** Hyphens only (no periods or underscores). Use `beesmart-avatar-<slug>-v3` (see table).

**Description:** Use the quirky, fun descriptions below (max 200 characters). Name max 55 characters — "Avatar" optional.

## Notes

- **Product ID** (step 1): `beesmart.avatar.<slug>.v3` — same as iOS.
- **Purchase option ID** (step 2): `beesmart-avatar-<slug>-v3` — hyphens only (required by Google).
- Backend accepts both formats for verification.
- **Android app:** When making purchases, pass these exact product IDs to Google Play Billing.
- **Subscription** uses `premium_monthly` as in `config/android.json`.
- **Name** max 55 characters (no "Avatar" required).
- **Description** max 200 characters — use the quirky in-app copy above.
- Product IDs cannot be changed or reused after creation.
- **Price summary:** 20 avatars at $0.99, 16 avatars at $1.99. In Google Play Console, set each product's price in step 2 (Availability and pricing).
- **New AAB:** Yes. Create new v3 products in Google Play Console and App Store Connect, deploy the updated backend, then build a new AAB. The app fetches product IDs from the API at runtime, so the new build will receive v3 IDs.
