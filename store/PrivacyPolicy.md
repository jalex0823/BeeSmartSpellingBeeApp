# Privacy Policy — BeeSmart Spelling Bee

Effective date: October 31, 2025

BeeSmart Spelling Bee (the “App”) helps learners practice spelling with kid‑friendly definitions and optional voice input. We respect your and your child’s privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

## Summary (Plain Language)
- We do not show third‑party ads and we do not sell personal information.
- We collect only what’s needed to power features like uploads, OCR, and voice input.
- If accounts are enabled for parents/teachers, we collect minimal account info.
- You can request data deletion at any time.

## What We Collect
- User Content: Word lists you upload (CSV/TXT/DOCX/PDF/images via OCR) and quiz results (correct/incorrect counts, streaks). Session data is primarily stored server‑side under a random session ID.
- Device Permissions: Microphone (for voice answers), Camera/Photos (for OCR uploads). Permission prompts appear only when using those features.
- Diagnostics (Optional): Crash logs and basic usage to improve reliability.
- Account Info (If enabled): Parent/teacher email/username and basic profile. Kids are not asked to provide personal info.

## How We Use Data
- Provide core app features (upload, OCR, dictionary lookups, voice input, quizzes, and progress views)
- Maintain app security, prevent abuse, and improve performance
- Support requests you send us

## Children’s Privacy (COPPA)
- The App is designed for children’s learning and may be distributed under Kids/Designed for Families programs.
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without verifiable parental consent.
- If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Data Sharing
- We do not sell personal data. We do not share data with third‑party advertisers.
- Service providers (e.g., cloud hosting) may process data on our behalf under appropriate contracts and security commitments.

## Security
- We use HTTPS for data in transit and restrict access on a need‑to‑know basis. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect data.

## Data Retention
- Session data is retained for active practice and cleaned periodically.
- If accounts are enabled, account data is retained while the account remains active or as required by law.
- You can request deletion (see “Your Rights”).

## Your Rights and Choices
- Access/Deletion: Request a copy of your data or deletion of your account/content.
- Parental Controls: Parents/guardians can manage a child’s data; we may require verification.
- Opt‑out of optional diagnostics by contacting support.

## International Users
- Data may be processed in the United States or other regions where our providers operate. We apply appropriate safeguards for international transfers when required.

## Changes
- We may update this policy. We’ll post changes here with an updated “Effective date.”

## Contact
- Email: privacy@beesmartspelling.app
- Website: https://beesmartspelling.app/privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about your child’s information, please contact us.
