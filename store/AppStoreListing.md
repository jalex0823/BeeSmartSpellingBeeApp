- Description:

BeeSmart Spelling Bee makes spelling practice simple and fun. Create your own word banks by uploading files (CSV/TXT/DOCX/PDF) or photos of worksheets using built‑in OCR. Every word comes with a kid‑friendly definition, example sentence, and optional phonetic spelling so learners can hear and see how words are formed—without revealing the answer.

The quiz is designed for focus. Kids can answer by typing or speaking, ask for a hint, and track streaks as they improve. For parents and teachers, it’s easy to import lists and get students practicing right away—at home or in class.

Highlights:

- Multi‑format uploads: CSV, TXT, DOCX, PDF; plus photos with OCR
- Kid‑friendly definitions and examples; optional phonetic spelling
- Voice or keyboard input; hints without showing the word
- Progress tracking: correct/incorrect counts and streaks
- Ad‑free, privacy‑minded, and classroom‑ready
